About

{% include responsive-image.html src="/assets/images/profile.jpg" alt="Portrait of Andrew Erbs" width="400" height="400" loading="lazy" %}

I’m interested in the gap between how systems are supposed to work and how people actually behave in them. That thread runs through eight years as a Navy surface warfare officer, product work at Amazon, research at Yale’s Center for Customer Insights, and the projects above.

I studied computer engineering and philosophy at Penn State, and since September 2026 I have led applied AI work at GO Media in Raleigh.
